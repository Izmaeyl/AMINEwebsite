Privacy policy

This notice describes the current AMINE preview storefront operated by THE AMINE PERFUMES. For privacy questions, contact support@theamineparfums.com.

Your shopping bag

The site saves product identifiers and quantities in your browser’s local storage under “amine-bag” so your bag remains available on this device. This bag data is not submitted as an order. You can remove individual products, clear your bag below, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Messages you send us

If you contact us by email or social media, we receive the contact details and message you choose to send. We use them to respond to your enquiry. Please do not send payment-card details or sensitive personal information in a message. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us.

Hosting and external services

This storefront is hosted through Vercel. Hosting and access services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information and access logs to deliver and protect the site. Their own privacy terms apply to those services. Social links open external platforms; those platforms process information under their own privacy policies.

Analytics and payments

This storefront currently installs no Google Analytics or advertising tags and has no payment checkout, account-registration or newsletter form. It does not collect shipping addresses or payment details. This notice will need updating when those features are introduced.

Keeping and deleting information

Your saved bag remains in this browser until removed or cleared. Contact messages are kept as needed to handle the enquiry and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Write to our support email with questions or deletion requests.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page as the storefront and its services change.
